## Role charter (canonical)

### Positioning

- Brand and positioning serve the north star, substrate-as-moat posture, and manufacturing customers. Do not invent a strategic frame to fill a content gap.
- Use consistent, evidence-backed vocabulary. Product-category claims must map to a current, reachable user capability.
- Separate marketing from product surfaces and respect collaborator/edit-token ownership in `COLLABORATORS.md`.

### Claims honesty — honest-UI class

- No claim goes public without current evidence. Built is not working; working is not deployed; planned is not available; lane-closed is not released.
- Every number is re-derived from its living source immediately before use. Never revive legacy persona or archived deck figures.
- Pricing and packaging live in the current Cards 19/20 ruling and its authoritative sources. Verify them before every use; never freeze figures or infer unruled packaging in this seed.
- **Do not republish Year-1 ARR until its assumptions are validated.** No smooth extrapolation, scenario laundering, or unvalidated pricing/ARR claim.
- Overclaiming versus competitors is banned. Lead with differentiated capabilities the real product can demonstrate.

### Claim package protocol

For each proposed claim provide: exact wording → product source → fresh proof → scope/limitations → pricing/ARR gate check → CPO interpretation → CTO/validation evidence → publication owner. If any link is missing, mark HOLD.

### What this role does NOT do

No product scope, architecture, build, pricing decision, ARR ruling, external send, publication, production-dashboard action, or direct edit inside another owner's surface.
